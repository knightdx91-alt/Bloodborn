KayKit asset packs in this directory
=====================================
All packs below are by Kay Lousberg (KayKit), released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) — free for personal and
commercial use, no attribution required.
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Included (free tiers only):
- characters/: Character Pack Adventurers 1.0 (GitHub) — 5 rigged/
  animated characters (Barbarian, Knight, Mage, Rogue, Rogue_Hooded),
  76 clips each; Character Pack Skeletons 1.0 (GitHub) — 4 rigged/
  animated skeletons (Mage, Minion, Rogue, Warrior), 95 clips each.
- animations/: Character Animations 1.1 (itch.io free tier) —
  Rig_Medium animation library, 8 GLBs (CombatMelee, CombatRanged,
  General, MovementAdvanced, MovementBasic, Simulation, Special,
  Tools). Designed for the KayKit medium humanoid rig.
- dungeon/: Dungeon Remastered 1.0 (GitHub) — 200+ modular dungeon
  pieces and props.
- weapons/: Fantasy Weapon Bits 1.0 FREE (itch.io) — 25+ fantasy
  weapons (gltf+bin).
- halloween/: Halloween Bits 1.0 (GitHub) — spooky props (gltf+bin).
- furniture/: Furniture Bits 1.0 (GitHub) — interior furniture
  (gltf+bin).
- hexagon/: Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0 (GitHub) — 200+ medieval
  buildings/decoration/hex tiles (gltf+bin).
- forest/: Forest Nature Pack 1.0 FREE (itch.io) — trees, bushes,
  rocks and nature props (gltf+bin).
- medieval/: Medieval Builder Pack 1.0 (itch.io) — 226 building
  GLBs + hex/square tile sets.

All textures are single 1024x1024 gradient atlases (no downscale
needed for the phone prototype). EXTRA/SOURCE paid tiers were NOT
taken. Full pack originals (all formats) are archived in the
workspace game-assets library, not in this repo.

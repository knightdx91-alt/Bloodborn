# Bloodborn — PvE & Content Model (working canon v1)

STATUS: **WORKING CANON** (adopted 2026-07-26, delegated best-call).
Covers P8. The mortal world's content; the demigod realm is
`demigod-realm.md`.

---

## 1. No instanced PvE — the world is the dungeon

**Call: PvE lives in the shared world.** No instanced dungeons. The
blood pools in deep places — **poolings** — and around them grow
**delves**: cave systems, drowned crypts, ruin-warrens in the wedges.
Delves are persistent, shared, contested spaces:

- Shallow floors are soloable; depth scales danger and material
  quality. The deepest chambers of a delve want a coordinated group.
- Because delves are shared, the good ones are *political*: Houses
  post harvesting rights claims (warrable, L25), Companies clear
  routes for pay, rival groups race to a fresh pooling.
- Only war battles get instanced-ish treatment (L25); everything else
  happens in the one world. Scarcity of PvE space is a feature — it
  makes PvE part of the economy and the politics.

## 2. Monster ecology — the supply chain with teeth

Monsters are **blood-warped** (lore §2): ordinary creatures changed by
pooled blood. They are not spawn-timer decorations:

- **Territories and pressure.** Warped populations grow if unculled,
  pushing out of the wedges toward roads and farms. Cull contracts
  (below) are real maintenance, and Vigil Orders treat them as holy
  work (L24).
- **Monster parts are crafting materials** (L4) — every hunt is a
  supply run. Beast-handlers capture live specimens for the Ring's
  beast bouts (L6).
- **Named horrors.** A pooling left alone too long births something
  *specific* — a named creature with generated traits and a growing
  bounty. Server-history material (L23): the year the Hollow Sow broke
  the Thornfield hedges gets remembered.

## 3. Quests — paper and rumor, not exclamation marks

**Call: no quest-hub filler; no markers over heads.** Three content
layers:

1. **Handcrafted mystery chains** — the high bar set by awakening
   quests (L7): faith questlines, spell quests, delve mysteries, the
   secret canon's deniable traces (§9). Authored, discoverable,
   missable — being missable is what makes finding one an event.
2. **Contract boards** — systemic work generated from *actual world
   state*: cull contracts on real monster populations, escort
   contracts for real caravans (often player-posted), commissions
   (L26), delve-clearing bids, harvest labor at rights sites. The
   board never lies about the world; if the contract exists, the
   problem exists.
3. **Rumor** — content discovery *is* the fireside story (L22).
   NPCs trade rumors shaped by epoch and recent server history;
   some are stale, some are wrong, some are the thread that ends in
   an awakening. Buying drinks is a search interface.

## 4. The shape of a life (content journey)
Start in a town (chosen at creation — your accent, your neighbors,
your first contracts). Local work → first wedge trips → a trade or a
banner (or both) → delve depths, wars, mastery, maybe the quiet
discovery that changes everything → faith, rep, and — for the few who
cap the climb — the Grave. Skill-by-use (L18) means the "content" of
leveling is just *doing the life*; the game never hands you a
theme-park rail, because the Wheel itself is the ride.

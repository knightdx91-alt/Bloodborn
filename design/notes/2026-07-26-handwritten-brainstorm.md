# Handwritten brainstorm — 2026-07-26 (transcription)

Faithful transcription of the original notebook page. Source of truth for
the founding ideas; expansions live in `design/brainstorm.md`.

**Game ideas — areas in a circle**

- 6 mid-size towns arranged around one very large capitol building
- Crafting
- Able to buy buildings, or rights to use them. There are various options
  they can use the buildings for (shop, brothel, etc.)
- Large arena in capitol (PvP & PvM), players can bet on outcomes
- Have to do quests to unlock mage abilities
- Players can teach spells, as trainer, but must reach mastery rank in
  that spell
- In large-scale wars: losing players lose 1 random piece of equipped
  equipment
- Mounted combat
- Religious factions; once max rep & max level, able to import to new
  area → pure PvP, you have become a demigod. There are creatures that
  can kill you, as well as players. Permadeath from now on, but when you
  die you get something (TBD). Only way to advance to new area is by
  killing other players as well as monsters.

**Platform targets:** full 3D — PC, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch.

**Direction note:** crafting should be *really involved*.

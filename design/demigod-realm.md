# Bloodborn — The Interior (demigod realm, working canon v1)

STATUS: **WORKING CANON** (adopted 2026-07-26, delegated best-call).
Covers P10. Spoiler-adjacent: builds on `lore.md` §9 secret canon.

---

## 1. Shape: the Wheel, inverted

The mortal world is a wheel around the Grave; the realm beyond the
gate is its dark mirror — **descent instead of spread**. Four rings,
named for a body, because it is one:

1. **The Skin** — entry ring. Four faith-gates open here, one per
   faith, each into a different region with that faith's blessing on
   its ascendants. Largest ring, most demigods, "safest" (nothing is
   safe).
2. **The Veins** — rivers that remember being blood; the landscape
   moves. Fewer, stronger, richer.
3. **The Marrow** — the deep structure. The god's dreams are load-
   bearing here; the enemy's fingerprints (lore §9) are legible for
   those who look.
4. **The Heart** — still beating. The end of the game's longest road.

Founding rule preserved **[core]**: permadeath everywhere, creatures
that can kill you as well as players, and the only way forward is
through killing — players and monsters both.

## 2. Advancement: ichor — kills made physical

**Call: kills yield ichor** — condensed god-blood, the realm's only
currency and its only key.

- Every kill — demigod or deep-warped creature — leaves ichor. Bigger
  life, more ichor.
- **Ichor is carried, never banked** (the physicality rule, L26/L28,
  followed to the bottom). Kill a demigod, take everything they
  carried. The rich are the hunted.
- **Descending costs ichor**: each ring's gates take a steep offering.
  Advancement is therefore literally the founding note — you go
  deeper only over the dead.
- Spent ichor feeds the seal (lore §9). The realm's economy IS the
  ward's maintenance — nobody inside knows.

## 3. Entropy: the Interior always wins

**Call: no crafting, no repair, no shops beyond the gate.** Gear
degrades (L3) and cannot be restored — only scavenged from the dead.

- Every demigod is on a clock made of their own equipment. Camping
  the Skin forever isn't viable; the realm grinds everyone toward
  their final fight.
- This keeps Ascendant seats (L10), sainthoods, and Monument lines
  cycling without any artificial timer. Entropy is the timer.
- Alliances are legal and unenforceable — pacts, betrayals, and
  shared descents are the realm's politics. Any combat goes.

## 4. The Heart (endgame of the endgame)

The first demigod to pay the Marrow's full price and reach the Heart
learns the truth (lore §9: the god chose this; the enemy is real; the
seal is everyone) — and gets the choice:

- **Join the pantheon.** The character retires forever into godhood:
  a permanent, server-visible mark — a blessing, a holiday, a change
  of light — *named after them*, plus the grandest Monument line and
  sainthood. The seal strengthens. This is launch's canonical ending.
- **The other option** is written but sealed (expansion arc: the one
  who cracks the door). Launch players sense a refusal is possible;
  what it does is not in any launch data.

The Heart-reacher keeps the truth: no system broadcasts what they
learned. Whether they tell their server — or lie — is theirs.

## 5. What ascension grants (sketch for tuning)
Crossing the gate: the demigod frame — a hard step up in body skills
(L18 caps lift), a faith-gate blessing, and senses tuned to ichor and
to other demigods' presence. Enough to feel transcendent; not enough
to ignore three mortals' worth of skill ganging up — moderate
vertical (L19) holds even here.

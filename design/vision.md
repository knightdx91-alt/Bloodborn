# BLOODBORN — Vision

**A large-scale, cross-platform 3D MMORPG (PC, Xbox, PlayStation,
Switch — one shared world, full crossplay) where the world keeps the
score.** Buy-to-play. M-rated grounded dark fantasy. Optional
companion mobile app for the life you leave running while you're away.

---

## The pitch

A god died at the center of the world, and the world grew like a
wheel around its grave: six great towns ringing one vast capitol.
Its blood is in the land — it knits the dying back to their feet, it
warps beasts in the deep places, and in a few people, in secret, it
*quickens*.

You start with none of that. You start with a trade, a horse, and a
town that learns your name. Bloodborn is a world of real things:
every sword was forged by a player whose mark it carries, every
price was set by a shopkeeper, every cart of iron physically crossed
dangerous country because nothing — goods or people — teleports.
Wars are declared contracts over real stakes, fought by the enrolled,
where the dead drop gear on the field. An arena takes bets. A
monument takes names.

And magic? Magic is a fireside story. Until the night you see
someone close a wound with a word — or do it yourself — and learn
the story is true, and that three secret institutions hunt everyone
who learns it. Spells are found, mastered, and taught person to
person; every spell on the server traces to whoever discovered it;
one living Incarnate may embody each spell, and their seat changes
hands in a midnight arena free-for-all.

At the top of the longest climb, the faithful may pass the one gate
that never opens twice. Beyond it: the Interior — permadeath,
demigods, and a descent paid for in the blood of everything you
kill. No one comes back. The fallen become saints and monument
lines. The first to reach the still-beating Heart learns why the
god died — and becomes a god the whole server lives under, by name.

**Every server writes its own history.** The myth of magic erodes at
the pace its players break it; wars, saints, spell lineages, and the
one player who unveils magic to the world are permanent, named
record. Two servers a year apart are different worlds with different
legends.

## Five promises

1. **Made by hands** — a fully player-driven economy of physical
   goods, deep discovered crafting, and maker's marks that make
   craftsmen famous.
2. **Distance is real** — no fast travel, no item mail; mounts,
   caravans, and coach rides make geography, trade, and escort work
   matter.
3. **Secrets are real** — magic hidden even from the marketing;
   knowing isn't having; discovery spreads player to player.
4. **Loss is real** — a death ladder with teeth: durability → war
   loot → permadeath godhood. Destruction feeds the crafting economy
   forever.
5. **History is real** — living epochs, named legends, a monument
   that never forgets, and an ending players can actually reach.

## Played on anything, tended from anywhere

BotW-style real-time action combat, controller-first, on all four
platforms in one shared world. The **companion app** covers the
asynchronous life: check your shop's ledgers and restock orders,
adjust prices, answer commission bids, read the rumor mill and the
Monument feed, follow war declarations and arena odds. Never
gameplay-critical, never a second job — the shopkeeper's evening
glance, not a raid scheduler.
